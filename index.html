🎯 What it is

Most players try to improve simply by playing more, and unknowingly repeat the same mistakes. Quality coaching is expensive and hard to find.

This project is an AI coach that gives every player a detailed review after each match: what went wrong, why it happened, and what to practice next.

🔍 How it works

  1. 1

    You share your gameplay

    Upload a match recording or enter your match stats. Pick your game and your role.

    What this means: You just give the coach your match. No setup, no coding.
  2. 2

    The AI analyzes it

    It looks at where you landed, when you rotated, how you fought, and how you used your loadout and utilities.

    What this means: The AI watches your game like a human coach would and notes every key decision.
  3. 3

    You get a coach’s review

    Plain-language feedback with the moments that mattered, so you see the mistake and the better option.

    What this means: Not only a score. You are told what went wrong, in simple words.
  4. 4

    You follow a training plan

    Daily drills for aim, positioning, and decisions, adjusted as your results improve.

    What this means: Short daily practice aimed at your weak spots, not random grinding.
Example feedback

“You pushed into the open at the third zone with 40% health. Holding the compound and healing first would have kept your squad alive.”

❓ Questions

How will the AI train me?

After each match, the AI shows you what went wrong and gives you a simple daily plan. The plan includes aim drills, zone timing practice, and teamwork habits. As your results improve, the plan changes with you.

Who teaches the AI?

The AI learns from the gameplay of T1 players. This helps it understand how top players land, rotate, fight, and make decisions in both BGMI and Free Fire.

Where does the advice come from?

The advice is shaped by T1 coaches, so you get the same kind of guidance that pro teams use. It is explained in simple words that every player can follow.

Will the advice be the same for everyone?

No. The AI looks at your own matches and your role, such as IGL, Entry, Support or Sniper. It then gives advice that fits the way you play.

Can the AI answer my own questions?

Yes. Ask the AI Coach anything, for example “How do I win close fights in the final circle?” and you will get a clear answer with steps you can practise today.

Which games will it support?

BGMI and Free Fire first. More eSports games will follow after that.

Will it replace human coaches?

No. It’s a coach you can use any time, for any match, at no extra cost per session. Human coaches can still do things an AI can’t.

Will there be tournaments?

Yes, that is the plan. Weekly and monthly tournaments with trophies and prize money in ₹, and a chance for top players to play with T1 players. Details will come as the app gets closer.

Is it free?

I haven’t decided yet. The project is still in planning.

When will it launch?

There’s no date yet. I’ll share progress as I build.
